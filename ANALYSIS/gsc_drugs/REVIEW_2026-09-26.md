## 1. What the screen asks and how

**Question:** which DSigDB drug gene sets line up with the tumour stem-cell (GSC) programs, and which oppose the recurrence change inside those programs. The code is `C:/Users/grego/OneDrive/Desktop/u251-xenograft-murine-RNASeq-study/ANALYSIS/gsc_drugs/gsc_screen.py` (identical to the grid copy). Both jobs completed with exit 0.
- Job 40467921 wrote `G/results/` (Varn sets only). Its 3,588 rows are identical to the matching rows of job 40468077 (maximum difference 0).
- Job 40468077 wrote `G/results_published/`, which adds the published sets.

**Inputs**
- Gene sets: `hs.dsigdb.v1.0.gmt`.
- Differential expression: `results_therapy_v3/.../therapy_impact.deseq2.results.tsv`, 3 recurrent (IL66B, NL70B, NL71B) vs 3 primary (IL67B, IL68B, IL69B).
- Human TPM: `results_human_final/star_salmon/salmon.merged.gene_tpm.tsv`.
- "Expressed" means median TPM ≥ 1 over the six tumours: 12,394 genes. The ORA universe is expressed genes that appear in DSigDB: 10,223 (`G/results_published/gsc_screen_log.json`).

**GSC sets** (expressed genes in brackets; citations from `ANALYSIS/gsc_drugs/refs_online/gene_set_index.tsv`)
- **Varn 2022** (Cell 185:2184, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2022.04.038, Table S3): genes whose `stemcell_tumor` or `prolif_stemcell_tumor` column is at least twice every other column. stem [30], prolif_stem [191], union [221].
- **Suva 2014** (Cell 157:580, PMID 24726434): core-TF targets [165]; 19 TPC-specific TFs [8].
- **Richards 2021** (Nat Cancer 2:157, PMID 35122077): Developmental vs Injury-Response GSCs [1,755]. This compares two GSC subtypes, not GSCs against differentiated cells.
- **Couturier 2020** (Nat Commun 11:3406, PMID 32641768): top 200 DC3 genes [192], mostly cell-cycle genes.
- **Bhaduri 2020** (Cell Stem Cell 26:48, PMID 31901251): radial-glia-like cluster markers [1,372]. Each cluster was compared with all atlas cells, non-malignant cells included.
- **MSigDB:** Beier CD133+ UP [26], Harris [23], Guenther spherical UP [10].

**Two screens**
- **S1:** hypergeometric over-representation per DSigDB term, BH q, effect = log2 odds ratio. It has no direction.
- **S2:** gseapy preranked GSEA (1,000 permutations, set sizes 5–500) using the statistic the published chain used, sign(log2FC) × −log10 p. The ranked list is restricted to the GSC genes. Sets with fewer than 30 genes were skipped: Suva TFs, Guenther, Harris and Beier got S1 only.
- Terms with q < 0.25 then get ChEMBL phase and ADMET-AI BBB_Martins, and are ranked by |effect|^1.5 × BBB.

## 3. Relation to slide 9: consistent with it, not independent of it

I re-ran the whole-transcriptome GSEA to see which DSigDB terms sit behind slide 9 (`SCR/recompute.py`). My values reproduce the published ones:

| Term | My NES | Published NES |
|---|---|---|
| ciclopirox MCF7 UP | −2.26 | −2.261 |
| ciclopirox PC3 UP | −2.14 | −2.156 |
| deferoxamine MCF7 UP | −2.39 | −2.39 |
| pentetrazol HL60 UP | −1.92 | −1.946 |
| nilutamide PC3 UP | −1.93 | −1.884 |
| primidone PC3 UP | −1.81 | −1.866 |
| pyrantel HL60 UP | −1.77 | −1.792 |

All of slide 9's top five are drug-induced ("UP") gene sets. They fall into two gene blocks (`SCR/leading_edge.py`, `SCR/le2.py`):
- **Ciclopirox and deferoxamine are a hypoxia block.** 21 of ciclopirox MCF7 UP's 36 leading-edge genes and 13 of deferoxamine's 15 are in HALLMARK_HYPOXIA. That hallmark itself falls in recurrence (NES −1.96).
  - With the 200 hypoxia genes removed, ciclopirox drops to NES −1.57 (p 0.005). It survives, weakened.
  - Deferoxamine drops to NES −1.45 (p 0.074, 7 genes left).
- **Pentetrazol, nilutamide, primidone and pyrantel are one signal counted four times.**
  - Their leading edges overlap heavily (pairwise Jaccard 0.55–0.86).
  - Each is 13–21 genes, 6–7 of them ribosomal-protein genes (RPL38, RPL14, RPL37A, RPS11, RPL27A, RPS10, RPLP2), plus PGK1 and ENO1.
  - So they are the ribosomal-protein block again. The slide-9 note that ciclopirox is not the ribosomal block is correct, but ranks 2–5 are.

The GSC screen finds ciclopirox only because the Bhaduri marker set contains 82 hallmark-hypoxia genes, including 47 genes of ciclopirox MCF7 UP. The stem-specific sets (Varn stem, Suva, Beier, Harris, Guenther) give no drug in slide 9's direction.

Ciclopirox's suppressed genes (MCF7 DOWN, 32 mitotic genes: MKI67, TOP2A, AURKA, PLK1, …) rise in recurrence: NES +2.13 on the whole transcriptome, not scored by the published NES < 0 filter. That also fits "opposes recurrence", but etoposide, trifluridine and resveratrol show the same, so it is not specific to ciclopirox.

**Caution for Q&A.** Ciclopirox's induced genes are HIF targets, so "opposing recurrence" here means pushing hypoxia/HIF genes back up. HIF signalling is linked to glioma stem-cell maintenance (Li Z et al., Cancer Cell 2009;15:501; I cited this from memory, so verify before quoting). The team's own literature table lists Pádua 2023 (Cancers 15:4406, PMID 37686684): ciclopirox raised stemness markers (SOX2, HIF1A) in gastric cancer cells. The same table records no GSC-selectivity test for ciclopirox (Su 2021) (`refs_online/gsc_selective_compounds_literature.tsv`).

**Side observation, not checked by leave-one-out or hold-out.** On the whole transcriptome the proliferative and stem programs are higher in recurrence: Varn prolif-stem NES +2.09, Varn stem +1.55, Beier +1.56, G2M +2.10. The radial-glia-like set is lower (−2.00). These p-values come from a gene-permutation null and are nominal (`SCR/gsc_sets_in_recurrence.py`). Do not put them on a slide without the leave-one-out and hold-out checks (STORY_v4 item 9).

## 4. Defects

The raw S1/S2 tables are sound. The final ranking built from them is not.

1. **Drug names are never parsed** (`gsc_screen.py:53`). The code splits on "_", but DSigDB v1.0 terms use spaces, so each "drug" is a whole term such as "deferoxamine mcf7 up".
   - ChEMBL was queried 2,672 times with those strings, taking the first free-text hit.
   - Deferoxamine was mapped to CHEMBL4635234 (phase NaN); the correct entry is CHEMBL556, phase 4 (`drug_rematch/chembl_mapping.csv`).
   - Pentetrazol was mapped to CHEMBL201776 (phase NaN); the correct entry is CHEMBL116943, phase 2.
   - Both are therefore missing from `gsc_screen_clinical_ranked.tsv`. Overall, 2,268 of 5,354 hits have no phase (`G/results_published/gsc_screen_hits.tsv`).
2. **Division by zero in the odds ratio** (`:133`). When the overlap equals the set size, log2 OR comes out near 45. This happens for Pomalidomide CTD (5/5), ammonia CTD (6/6), IPRODIONE (5/5) and VALPROIC ACID CTD (10/10). Three of these are the top three of the clinical ranking, with scores 265–289.
3. **Two scales mixed in one score** (`:167`). The score combines S1 log2 OR (0–9) with S2 NES (1–2.4), so S1 supplies 2,686 of the 2,752 ranked rows. S1 has no direction, so a drug's UP and DOWN sets both count.
4. **Small or off-target gene sets.** S2 skipped four sets for size, and Varn stem allowed only 14 terms. The two large sets are not GSC-vs-differentiated contrasts (Richards compares GSC subtypes; Bhaduri compares against the whole atlas and carries hypoxia genes).
5. **Minor points.**
   - The S2 "overlap" column is the leading-edge count, not the set size.
   - S2 used minimum set size 5, against 15 in the published chain.
   - No correction was made across the 7 S2 runs.
   - "p = 0" means less than 1/1000.
   - DSigDB symbols were not HGNC-repaired: 1,623 of 19,512 are missing from the TPM gene names, at least 254 of them old-style names. The published chain has the same loss.

**No other defects.** No output is empty, and there is no gene-ID mismatch: S2 ranked 1,754 of 1,755 Richards genes and 1,372 of 1,372 Bhaduri genes.

**Recomputed checks** (`SCR/recompute.py`):
- **S1:** ciclopirox MCF7 DOWN × varn_prolif_stem, with N = 10,223, K = 46, n = 182, x = 32, gives p = 1.177e-46 and log2 OR 7.2554 (Fisher 1.1775e-46). The file has 1.177471e-46 and 7.255429, an exact match.
- **S2:** ciclopirox MCF7 UP within Bhaduri gives NES −2.09 against −2.084 in the file. Deferoxamine MCF7 UP gives −1.97 against −1.924, and ciclopirox PC3 UP −1.78 against −1.767. These agree within permutation noise.

## 5. Recommendation: leave it out; add two note lines

It adds no independent evidence, the stem-specific sets are empty in slide 9's direction, and its ranked output has defects 1–3.
