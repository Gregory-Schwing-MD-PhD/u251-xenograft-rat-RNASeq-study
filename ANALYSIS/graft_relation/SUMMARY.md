## Is the graft % related to the results?

Yes, but not through contamination. The recurrent samples contained less human tissue (graft 29–43 % against 43–64 % in the primaries), and several per-sample scores rise with graft %. With six animals, "recurrence" and "less tumour in the piece sequenced" cannot be told apart statistically.

Rat reads leaking into the human data are not the cause. The leak lands in conserved non-coding RNA and moves the translation genes by less than 0.01 log2, against −0.40 observed; for AC it pushes the wrong way. The likelier link is what each sample contained: how much tumour, edge versus core, and how much brain came with it. Because LITT probably causes the smaller regrowth, graft % may be part of the treatment effect rather than a nuisance to adjust away.

Result by result:
- **Translation fall:** at gene level it does not depend on graft %, and adjustment strengthens it on every count-based measure. The apparent graft dependence came from scoring un-normalised TPM. Most of its size comes from one primary tumour, IL68B.
- **AC fall:** it survives adjustment and appears in the one graft-matched pair (NL70B vs IL67B, −0.61), so it is the most defensible group difference. Its p-values are parametric only, and CIBERSORT points the other way.
- **MES fall:** it follows sample composition as closely as it follows group (MES1 r −0.92 with m). MES2 is IL66B alone.
- **Ciclopirox:** there is no per-sample group or graft signal (GSVA +0.05, p 0.84; r with graft −0.06). Its first place comes from a gene-ranking enrichment that depends on IL66B.

## How to say it in the talk

- **Translation fall.** Suggested wording: "At gene level, ribosomal and translation-initiation genes were lower in recurrent tumours (mean log2FC −0.40). Rat contamination cannot produce this and graft adjustment does not remove it, but most of its size comes from one primary tumour." Present the six gene sets as one signal, since they are one ~80-gene RPL/RPS block. Do not say LITT suppresses translation. IL68B is also a library-composition outlier: 7SK/7SL 10.1 % and RP 4.87 %, against 2.76–3.27 % RP in the others.
- **AC fall.** Suggested wording: "The AC-like programme was lower in recurrence, including at matched graft fraction." Call it the strongest group difference, and call it exploratory. It is 3 vs 3, the exact permutation floor is p 0.10, and CIBERSORT gives the opposite direction.
- **MES.** Suggested wording: "MES-like scores were lower in recurrent samples, but they track how much tumour each sample contained, and six samples cannot separate the two." MES2 is one tumour (IL66B) and should not be shown as a group result.
- **Ciclopirox.** Present it as a candidate that generates a hypothesis, not a finding. Without IL66B it drops from rank 1 to 3 (weighted) or 14 (unweighted), and its NES goes from −2.26 to −1.64. No per-sample score supports it.
- **NEU, if shown.** It is the one signature the rat leak can reach: the leak could account for 25–38 % of its rise.

## The experiment that separates recurrence from tumour burden

The design is a burden-matched primary reference, with a non-thermal control arm and a readout that excludes host cells:
- **Primary reference.** Implant primaries and harvest them across a range of MRI volumes, including small lesions matched to recurrence size, so graft % spans about 25–65 %. Fit each score against graft % in the primaries alone, then ask whether the recurrences fall off that curve. This tests "different at equal burden" without adjusting for something LITT may cause.
- **Control arms.** Add a non-thermal cytoreduction arm (partial resection or focal radiation, regrowth harvested at matched volume) and a sham-fibre arm. These separate regrowth after cytoreduction from thermal injury.
- **Readout.** Use FACS-sorted human cells or human-specific spatial probes, and compare tumour cells at matched local density, core vs edge. Differences that persist at matched density support LITT; differences that vanish support burden or niche.
- **Size.** With r(group, covariate) ≈ 0.7, about 15 animals per group are needed to detect a 1.5-SD effect at 80 % power, not 3.

Three cheaper pieces can start now:
- the tissue-weight and MRI-volume correlation above;
- the host-stream deconvolution (job 40468032, running): excess normal brain in the recurrences points to dissection or burden, while excess myeloid or reactive-astrocyte signal points to an LITT injury niche;
- graded sublethal hyperthermia of U251 in vitro, to ask whether heat alone lowers RP, AC or MES programmes.

## Not known

- Whether graft % tracks tumour burden, dissection margin or library insert size (r 0.91 with fragment length). Six samples cannot separate them.
- Whether reads from non-brain rat cells (microglia, macrophages, endothelium) leak into the human stream. The brain controls do not capture that profile.
- Why IL66B's library carries excess conserved-sequence reads.
